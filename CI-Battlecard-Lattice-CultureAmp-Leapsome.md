## 1. The three durable QW advantages (they hold against all three)

These are the wedges that survive competitor release cycles. Lead with these.

**1. True Recognition & Rewards — a package none of the three actually sell.**
All three have *recognition-lite* (Lattice "Praise", Culture Amp "Shoutouts", Leapsome "Instant Feedback & Praise / Praise Wall") — peer/manager shout-outs, often value-tagged, shared to Slack/Teams. **None has a rewards economy**: no points, no rewards catalog/marketplace, no redemption, no incentives. QW's `Recognition & Rewards` package (real-time, values-based recognition **+ actual rewards**) is a whole category the others don't play in. Highest-confidence, most concrete differentiator — and there's no roadmap signal that any of them is moving into rewards.

**2. Predictive retention (Retention Radar) — none has a true equivalent.**
QW's `Engagement` package includes AI/predictive analytics that flag flight risk and *advise on retention strategy* in-workflow. Lattice has **no named predictive-attrition product**. Leapsome is explicitly *reactive* ("Resilience Signals" = pattern flags, not a forward-looking risk score). Culture Amp is the closest — it has attrition modeling via its People Analytics (ex-Orgnostic) line — but positions it as **insight/analytics, not embedded manager action**. QW's edge is predictive risk *wired into the action loop*, strongest vs Lattice and Leapsome.

**3. A purpose-built, connected EX loop with an intact recognition link — and trust built in.**
QW's thesis is the survey → AI action plan → 1:1 → recognition → talent/succession-review loop running on one data model, with confidentiality/anonymity thresholds as a design non-negotiable. **Caveat, stated honestly:** "we're the connected platform" is no longer uncontested — all three now tell a connected-data + AI story (see §4). But each has a *broken link* QW doesn't: Lattice just **exited its own HRIS/payroll** and now rides a third-party system of record; Culture Amp's loop breaks at **recognition/rewards**; Leapsome is **spreading into full HRIS/HCM** and diluting EX focus. QW's four-package loop is intact, EX-purpose-built, and doesn't depend on someone else's backbone.

## 3. The competitors

### 3.1 Lattice — the AI-forward performance suite that walked away from core HR

**Snapshot.** Founded 2015 (San Francisco); performance-management heritage (reviews, goals, 1:1s) for modern tech/knowledge-work orgs. ~$3B valuation (2022). CEO Sarah Franklin (ex-Salesforce) is driving an AI-first "refounding." Sweet spot mid-market (~50–1,000 employees), pushing upmarket; US-centric with a real London/EMEA foothold. Transparent, public, modular per-seat pricing.

**Footprint by QW package.**
- `Engagement` — Moderate; priced cheapest ($4/seat). Pulse, eNPS, onboarding/exit, custom surveys; Mercer benchmarks; AI insights + action planning. Onboarding/exit only — no broad lifecycle-survey suite; **no named predictive-attrition product**.
- `Performance` — Deep; this is the core. Reviews (all directions), Calibration, PIPs, Goals & OKRs (cascading; Salesforce/JIRA sync), 1:1s, Feedback, Weekly Updates ("Habits").
- `Recognition & Rewards` — Recognition only. "Praise" (value-tagged shout-outs via Slack/Teams/email + analytics). **No rewards, points, catalog, or redemption.**
- `Development` — Covered but split. "Grow" (competency matrices, IDPs, career tracks, AI growth areas); **Talent Reviews + Succession Planning live under the Performance product.** No LMS/courseware.
- `Other / Cross-Platform` — The area they're betting the company on. **Lattice AI Agent** (OpenAI-based, included): real-time coaching, evidence-based review drafts, an AI agent in 1:1s, voice; **Lattice MCP** (pipes data into Claude/OpenAI/Slack, Jul 2026); **Workforce Intelligence / AI Leverage Insights** (ties people data + AI-usage to business outcomes). **Compensation** module (comp cycles, statements, equity, merit) — outside QW's scope, a Lattice advantage. 25+ integrations; Workday Partner Program (Oct 2025).

**Where they win (don't fight here).** Best-in-class performance UX (G2 ~4.4–4.5, 3,300+ 5-star). Credible, already-shipped agentic AI (AI-in-1:1s and evidence-based AI reviews are GA as of Jun–Jul 2026, not roadmap). Compensation (QW lacks it). OKR depth. Transparent modular pricing. Board-level "prove AI's ROI on performance" narrative.

**QW's durable edge vs Lattice.**
- **Recognition & Rewards** — Praise is social-only; QW has the full recognition **+ rewards** package.
- **Listening is a feature, not a heritage** — thinner lifecycle breadth and **no Retention Radar equivalent**; QW's listening → action loop and predictive risk win on QW's home turf.
- **They exited HRIS + payroll** — payroll ended 2026-03-31, HRIS ends 2026-07-31. A trust/continuity wedge: customers who standardized on Lattice as core HR are being **displaced and forced to migrate**, and Lattice's "connected" story now leans on Workday/others' system of record. QW's not-an-HCM posture is intact and non-disruptive.
- **Trust/anonymity** — Lattice doesn't foreground survey anonymity/confidentiality thresholds the way QW does (**Unknown / probe in-deal**).

**Trajectory (last 6–12 mo).** Agentic AI woven into performance workflows + "prove AI's business impact," with core-HR data *deliberately outsourced* to partners: Workday partnership (Oct 2025) → announced HRIS/payroll sunset (Nov 2025) → acquired Mandala (AI coaching, Mar 2026) → Workforce Intelligence + AI-in-1:1s + voice (Jun 2026) → evidence-based review drafts + MCP (Jul 2026).

**Verify before external use.** "Predictive attrition" is claimed by one third-party but **not on Lattice's own pages — treat as absent/soft**. Exact customer count ("5,000+ teams" vs trackers ~3,700). Anonymity depth Unknown.

### 3.2 Culture Amp — the people-science / benchmark incumbent, recognition-thin

**Snapshot.** Founded 2009 (Melbourne); the category-defining **employee-engagement / people-science** company ("People-led. AI-powered. Backed by science."). ~$1.5B valuation; claims ~25M employees across 6,000+ orgs. Mid-market → enterprise focus (sells down to SMB). Same buyer as QW (CHRO / Head of People / People Science). Sold as three modules — **Engage · Perform · Develop** — plus a People Analytics layer and AI Coach across all.

**Footprint by QW package.**
- `Engagement` — Deep; their crown jewel. 40+ survey templates, pulse, onboarding/exit, DEI; the differentiator is **benchmarking** (comparison vs ~1.6B data points, continuously expanded); AI comment summaries/sentiment; expert/proven action plans; a Performance Culture Diagnostic linking engagement × performance. Meets or exceeds QW on listening depth and benchmark dataset.
- `Performance` — Moderate → deep, fast-improving (built on the 2019 Zugata acquisition; "more powerful Perform" refresh 2025–26). Reviews + calibration, Goals (incl. cascading + weighting), 1-on-1s, continuous feedback, 360s, development plans. Credible overlap with QW Performance; historically the up-sell bought after Engage.
- `Recognition & Rewards` — **Shallow; the biggest gap.** "Shoutouts" = peer recognition in Slack/Teams with value keywords (from the 2021 Disco acquisition). **No rewards engine, points, catalog, or redemption.**
- `Development` — Moderate, newer/lighter. Career paths & competencies, Skills Coach (micro-learning), development plans, Go1 content integration. **No evidenced dedicated 9-box talent-review or succession-planning module** (calibration sits in Perform for rating fairness). Partial vs QW Development.
- `Other / Cross-Platform` — AI Coach across the platform (survey next-steps, review-writing help, guided role-play) — but **positioned as "a partner to managers, not an autonomous decision-maker": it assists/summarizes/coaches, it doesn't act.** People Analytics (ex-Orgnostic, 2024): attrition modeling, org-network analysis, scenario/headcount planning. Broad HRIS ecosystem (Workday, BambooHR, SAP SF, ADP, UKG…), Slack/Teams, self-service SAML/SSO (May 2026).

**Where they win (don't fight here).** The **benchmark dataset + people science** — the hardest-to-replicate asset in an enterprise engagement bake-off. Listening depth + genuine People Analytics. Brand ("safe choice" for enterprise People teams; G2 category leader). AI grounded in proprietary science data (not generic LLM). Enterprise credibility + integration breadth.

**QW's durable edge vs Culture Amp.**
- **Recognition & Rewards** — Shoutouts is recognition-only; **no rewards.** Cleanest, most concrete win.
- **AI that acts + predictive-retention-in-workflow** — their AI assists/writes/coaches; QW competes on AI that drives manager **action** and predictive risk embedded in the loop (narrowing, but real).
- **Development completeness** — no evidenced succession/9-box; QW's talent reviews + succession are more complete on paper.
- **The connected loop's weakest link is recognition** — their survey → action → performance path is real and tightening, but it has **no recognition/rewards rung**; QW's four-package loop is intact end-to-end.

**Trajectory (last 6–12 mo).** Consolidating from engagement-first into a connected engage + perform + develop + analytics platform, with people-science-grounded **AI Coach as the connective tissue** (rolled across surveys → Perform → whole platform through 2026). Benchmark expansion (AI Effectiveness benchmarks, Aug 2026). People Analytics build-out. **Notably NOT moving into rewards.**

**Verify before external use.** SCIM support Unknown. Dedicated succession module not evidenced. Whether Shoutouts has any paid rewards add-on — no evidence, but confirm. Pricing is quote-based (third-party estimates ~$5–10/employee/mo — directional only).

### 3.3 Leapsome — the modular European people-enablement suite, moving up into HRIS

**Snapshot.** Founded 2016 (Berlin); bootstrapped until a 2022 Series A. Heritage = "**people enablement**" (performance + engagement + learning + OKRs in one tool). **Repositioning (2025–26) into "the only AI-native HR platform" spanning HRIS + Talent + AI** — a deliberate move up and out into system-of-record territory. Sweet spot startups → mid-market (~50–1,000+); **European/DACH strength** (GDPR, EU data residency in Germany, ISO 27001, EU AI Act alignment, DATEV). G2 4.8/5 (~2,200+ reviews). US-enterprise credibility is thinner.

**Footprint by QW package.**
- `Engagement` — Moderate. Engagement/pulse/onboarding/project/custom surveys, eNPS; driver analysis, benchmarks, heatmaps; **anonymous by default with confidentiality thresholds**; "Resilience Signals" (team-pattern flags); AI action plans. Solid modern stack, but **no predictive attrition** and lighter enterprise listening science; full lifecycle/exit not clearly productized.
- `Performance` — Deep; their strongest, most mature area. 360/self/peer/manager/upward reviews, templates + question banks, calibration + AI pre-reads (leniency/anomaly flags); **Goals & OKRs** with Goal Trees (cascading, dependencies, Jira); structured 1:1s/meetings + AI Notetaker; continuous feedback. (G2 note: calibration feels *less mature* than Lattice/Betterworks.)
- `Recognition & Rewards` — Shallow. "Instant Feedback & Praise" + a Praise Wall (visibility controls; Slack/Teams). **No points, catalog, redemption, or rewards economy** (confirmed on their own recognition-comparison page). Any monetary reward flows through the Compensation module, not peer recognition.
- `Development` — Mixed, and **LMS-strong (a QW gap).** Real LMS: AI-authored learning paths, quizzes, blended/live training, auto-enrollment, Leapsome Academy + a Learning Marketplace, SCORM/AICC/xAPI/CMI5, LinkedIn Learning. Competency frameworks tied to reviews. Calibration + 9-box present; **dedicated succession module not evidenced (Unknown/limited).**
- `Other / Cross-Platform` — Expanding **HRIS suite** (employee record, docs/e-sign, absence, time tracking [early access], onboarding, Payroll Prep + DATEV/ADP export, recruiting [early access], whistleblowing). **Six live AI agents** (Development Coach, Meeting Notetaker, PeopleOps Partner, Data Analyst, Culture Advisor, HR Helpdesk) + three coming; a cross-module **AI Copilot that takes actions with confirmation** (request absence, add a 1:1 talking point, create a goal). Slack/Teams/Google/Jira/SSO/ADP/DATEV integrations.

**Where they win (don't fight here).** Modularity + breadth-for-price (buy one module, add as you grow). **Real LMS depth** (beats QW when a buyer wants EX + a content LMS in one tool). Mature OKRs/Goal Trees. **European footprint & compliance** (EU/DACH deals, EU-HQ'd multinationals). AI-agent breadth & momentum. All-in-one including HRIS for one-vendor buyers.

**QW's durable edge vs Leapsome.**
- **Recognition & Rewards** — praise wall only; **no rewards economy.** Clean QW win.
- **Predictive retention** — no flight-risk modeling; QW's Retention Radar is a strong wedge.
- **Enterprise listening depth** — lighter survey science/normative benchmarking and fewer lifecycle/exit specifics; QW's listening → action loop positions as deeper.
- **US-enterprise credibility & focus** — European mid-market is their home turf; NA buyers report slower support, opaque pricing, more rigid modules. And by chasing HRIS/payroll/recruiting, Leapsome is **spreading across the full HR stack** — QW can position as the **purpose-built EX / connected-four-package specialist** vs a broadening generalist.

**Trajectory (last 6–12 mo).** Aggressive AI build-out (Copilot → a fleet of named agents; cross-module, action-taking, Apr 2026), AI woven into every module (Review Assistant pulls transcripts + history + goal progress; AI form/analytics builders), HRIS expansion, Compensation buildout. Net direction: **from "people enablement" to "AI-native HR platform / system of record," moving up-market into HRIS/HCM.**

**Verify before external use.** A reported $120M Series C is **uncorroborated (single low-authority aggregator) — do not cite**; only the 2022 Series A is verified. Headcount/customer counts vary by source. Exact per-seat price Unknown (quote-based). Succession module and full lifecycle surveys — limited/Unknown.

## 4. Cross-cutting cautions (read before building any external asset)

- **Don't lead with "we have better AI."** Lattice ships agentic AI (AI-in-1:1s, evidence-based reviews, MCP) and Leapsome fields six live AI agents; Culture Amp's AI is science-grounded. Lead instead with **AI that acts inside a connected EX loop that includes recognition and predictive retention** — the combination, not raw AI horsepower.
- **"Connectedness" is now contested.** All three tell a connected-data story. The durable, defensible framing is the *intact* loop (esp. the recognition/rewards rung) and **purpose-built EX focus** vs each rival's dilution: Lattice ditched its HRIS, Leapsome is chasing one, Culture Amp has no rewards rung.
- **Pick the fight per matchup** — vs Culture Amp don't argue benchmarks/people-science; vs Lattice don't argue performance polish, comp, or agentic AI; vs Leapsome don't argue LMS, OKRs, or EU compliance. Win on the three durable wedges (§1).
- **Confidentiality.** Never externalize QW's multi-package retention numbers. Use the concept only.
- **Freshness.** These vendors ship weekly; re-verify competitor specifics before customer-facing use and refresh this card ~quarterly (or when the monthly `/ci-report` flags a material move by one of the three).
