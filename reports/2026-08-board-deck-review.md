## 0. Verdict (TL;DR)

The deck's spine is sound. Its four big theses — direct competitors converging on connected AI platforms, Recognition & Rewards as a durable QW differentiator, HRIS consolidation as the real enterprise threat, and AI-natives resetting buyer expectations — are all supported by QW's own CI corpus and by outside evidence. The R&R differentiation claim is the strongest-evidenced thing in the deck (verified on vendor pages: none of Lattice / Culture Amp / Leapsome sells a rewards economy).

But it is not board-proof yet. It has (a) one seriously wrong row — Confirm, which stopped being a startup three months ago; (b) a handful of checkable number/date errors (Culture Amp figures, Awardco MCP status, Culture Amp "draft reviews" timing); (c) internal contradictions between the quadrant slides, the matrices, and the takeaway text that a careful reader will spot in the room; and (d) missing the two biggest storylines of the last 30 days — the reported Workday/Silver Lake take-private talks and the July MCP/agent wave that QW's own July CI run documented across BambooHR, Betterworks, Paylocity, and Cornerstone.
