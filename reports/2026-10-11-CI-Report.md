## 2. Package Brief — October & November 2026

### Top Themes
- **Cross-signal AI that pushes managers to act.** Culture Amp's Multi-signal Intelligence (turnover risk + survey hotspots → 1:1 topic), 15Five's Amaya answering across reviews/recognition/engagement/surveys, Workday Peakon's Talent Sentiment Agent, and Workday's Sana Coach (performance + Peakon sentiment) all aim AI at the exact "connected insight → manager action" ground QW competes on. This is the most direct challenge to QW's platform thesis in recent runs.
- **MCP is now table stakes.** Five roster competitors shipped MCP connectors to Claude/ChatGPT in-window: Culture Amp (expanded to engagement + reviews, 10-18), 15Five (10-08, self-serve any-tool 11-23), Lattice (write tools in Oct, HR-admin analytics 11-12), BambooHR (Claude 10-08, ChatGPT 11-24), UKG (Claude 10-20). Following July's wave (BambooHR, Leapsome, Betterworks, Paylocity), most of the roster now exposes people data to external assistants.
- **AI capture of manager conversations and work.** Lattice Meetings (AI Agent joins calls) and Auto-Updates/Evidence-based Review drafts from Jira/GitHub/Slack; 15Five's Meeting Assistant agent for 1:1 prep and follow-up. Review evidence is being harvested from the flow of work rather than written.
- **Action planning is the engagement battleground.** Culture Amp rebuilt Action Plans, Qualtrics shipped an Action Plan Details widget, Perceptyx added a "Users Without Action Plans" report, Peakon gave outcome drivers manager Focus Areas pages. Listening → action follow-through is getting instrumented.
- **Survey generalists move toward EX.** SurveyMonkey shipped Workday/BambooHR HRIS sync and scheduled recurring email pulses; Gallup opened a redesigned Access beta with inline Gallup AI.
