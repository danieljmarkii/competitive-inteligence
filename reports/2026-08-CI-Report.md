## OUTPUT 2 — PACKAGE BRIEF

### Top Themes (August 2026)

- **The MCP/agent layer is now write-enabled.** Betterworks' MCP server v0.74.0 shipped its first write tools (create/update goals from external AI assistants); SurveyMonkey put survey creation and response retrieval inside ChatGPT. The frontier moved from "AI can read our data" to "AI can act in our product from outside our UI."
- **Lattice is buying AI-native performance, not just building it.** Pando (2026-08-19) is its second AI-performance acquisition of 2026 (after Mandala), stacked on top of July's MCP GA and evidence-based review drafts — a sustained platform + AI-layer push on QW's Performance package.
- **AI governance is becoming a product surface.** Qualtrics shipped AI Admin (org-wide control of first-party AI use), following Paylocity's Ignite AI Hub pattern from July — a Trust/Privacy axis QW should watch given its confidentiality-first positioning.
- **Core Engagement and Recognition & Rewards feature work was quiet.** August activity concentrated in Performance workflows and cross-platform AI/admin; no competitor shipped a verified R&R change this window.
- **Coverage caveat: several public update surfaces degraded this month.** Leapsome's changelog moved to a login-walled in-app hub; 15Five's public running list lagged its own releases; Lattice's August roundup was not yet published at run date — August counts for those vendors are floors, not totals (see Source Health).
